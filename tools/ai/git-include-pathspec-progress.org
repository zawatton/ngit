#+TITLE: ngit remote-URL includes, attribute pathspecs and subdirectory commands

Baseline: 273be60.  Three isolated lanes add includeIf
hasconfig:remote.*.url conditions, attribute pathspec magic, and the
remaining subdirectory/pathspec behavior (ls-files, diff --relative, clean
and paths leaving the invocation directory).  Protocol v2 is designed
separately and gets its own ledger.  Fourteen criteria were defined before
implementation; S5 was added after the independent audits to cover their fixes
(three criteria after the first audit, S5.4 after the re-audit, S5.5 after the
final audit, S5.6 after its fifth review round, S5.7 after
the sixth, S5.8 after the seventh).  Original test sources remain unchanged.
The test-source guard in S4.1 compares the baseline with this objective's
commit 6a462c1 (changed on 2026-10-27: wave A later changed the shared suites
test/compare.sh and test/patch-suite.sh).

Each suite reports exact comparisons and intended differences separately.
An intended difference is a named diagnostic or security divergence with a
reason recorded in the suite; it never counts as an exact comparison.
